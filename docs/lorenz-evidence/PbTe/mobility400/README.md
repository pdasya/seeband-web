# 400 K PbTe deformation-potential inversion

Run python reverse.py with NumPy and SciPy. The archived output is results.txt. There is no fit. The chemical potential solves charge neutrality. Separate effective deformation potentials are recovered algebraically from each inferred drift mobility using Eq. (3) of Pei et al. divided by its Hall factor, with the Fermi integrals in Eq. (6). Eqs. (1)–(3): https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.5355#page=2. The parabolic Sigma channel is the alpha=0 limit, keeping the same absolute acoustic normalization as the L channel. The geometrical conductivity mass is the per-valley harmonic directional average. The density of final states in the relaxation time belongs to one valley; the total DOS counts the full multiplicity. DOS mass and acoustic scattering conventions also appear in Heng Wang's thesis (Eq. 3.50, pp. 57–58): https://thesis.caltech.edu/8424/1/Heng_Wang_Thesis_Final.pdf . Fan & Oganov, AICON2 Eq. 17 and Table 1, use the DOS per valley and reuse the reported deformation potentials with other material inputs: https://uspex-team.org/static/file/AICON2.pdf .

Fig. 3(a) of the Nature article supplies the observed electrical resistivity. The associated SI gives p, b, the text's light-hole fraction, masses, band shapes, anisotropies, elastic sound speed and D values. Sources: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature09996/figures/3 and https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/r4cjr-qn161/files/nature09996-s1.pdf#page=3 . The file resistivity/README.md describes the figure digitization. Independent 400 K constraints were computed by check_s1.py and band_factors.py in the preceding hall400 folder. Density labels differ for the measured Hall sample and S3(b). Inputs from S3(b) are a cross-check using adjacent nominal doping. The Fig. 3 resistivity describes the same published composition, with no claim of identical pellets. For the exact absolute mobility scale we use mass density from the ideal PbTe rock-salt cell a=6.46 Å and the longitudinal modulus C=rho_mass v_l²; recovered D scales as sqrt(C rho_electrical). The ratio is independent of both. Inverted D values are conditional effective parameters, not separate measurements.

The related inventor disclosure https://patents.google.com/patent/US9059364B2/en describes effective single bands without explicit degeneracy and gives different values (light 12, heavy 4.5 eV, b around 5). This is evidence that another parameterization circulated. It does not specify that the Nature 2011 curves were computed with that parameterization. D'Souza et al., https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.102.115204, resolve multiple electron–phonon channels for p-type PbTe. Their result qualifies a microscopic interpretation of the acoustic effective D's; it is not proof of how the 2011 figures were generated.
