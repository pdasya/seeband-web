# PbTe without fitting

Run python replay.py with numpy, scipy, pandas and matplotlib. Files ../ref49/inset/fractions_1K.csv and ../reference.csv are geometric digitizations of Pei SI Fig. S3(b) and Thesberg Fig. 6 from the preceding ref49 study. The comparisons are to published calculations. Temperatures are 300 to 850 K every 5 K. Output Lorenz numbers are in 10^-8 W ohm/K². wL, wSigma and wC are fractions of total electrical conductivity.

Sources: https://doi.org/10.1038/nature09996 (Eq. 3); https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/r4cjr-qn161/files/nature09996-s1.pdf (Fig. S3, Sec. 2, Eq. S2); https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-09734-4 (Kane acoustic moments); https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.95.125206 (Fig. 6). See also https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.91.085207 for the lead-chalcogenide acoustic Kane transport kernel.

The forward calculation fixes net holes at each T. Each L/C pocket uses reduced nonparabolicity kBT/(EC−EL), Sigma uses zero. The reduced acoustic transport distribution is 3(x+αx²)/[(1+2αx)²+2], normalized to x in the parabolic limit. The carrier count uses the integrated 3D DOS, proportional to (x+αx²)^(3/2), integrated against −df/dx. For independent equivalent valleys and intravalley acoustic deformation scattering the relative prefactor is Nv/(mcond D²). Valley multiplicities enter DOS masses once; ellipsoidal conductivity mass uses the harmonic directional average per valley. Common elastic factors cancel. This specifies the assumed transport closure independently of the reference curves. Pei's supplement gives the deformation potentials and assumes acoustic scattering but refers elsewhere for the detailed equations. No fitted amplitude or adjustment to conductivity shares.

The reported intraband Lorenz number corresponds to the closure in Pei S2. The comparison to Thesberg solid curves uses open-circuit thermal transport, adding the L–Sigma Seebeck variance and the variance between combined holes and electrons. This is an independent forward calculation outside the production application. The script verifies neutrality, the combined moment identity and quadrature convergence. At the 700 K points, electron share is below 0.13%; the inferred ratio of light- to heavy-hole mobility from digitized fractions is approximate when neglecting electrons.
