# PbTe band Hall factors at 400 K

Run python band_factors.py with NumPy and SciPy. Original formula and definitions: Pei, LaLonde, Wang and Snyder, Low effective mass leading to high thermoelectric performance, Eqs. (2),(6), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.5355#page=2. This later paper specifies the acoustic Kane kernel for n-PbTe. The reconstruction applies it to L holes, as a two-band Kane approximation using the direct C–L gap of 0.34 eV. Magnitudes only. Original inputs and test observable: https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/r4cjr-qn161/files/nature09996-s1.pdf#page=3. For parabolic Sigma set alpha=0 and K=1. Both ellipsoid and energy factors are included in A=e p R_H for a hole band.

The input eta values 1.9 and 1.3 are the refined estimates in Pei 2011. For sensitivity each band is also evaluated at its quoted approximate limits. The Hall peak inference solves stationary S1 algebraically with fixed factors and a locally constant drift mobility ratio. These are inferred values conditional on that assumption. At 2.5e20 cm^-3 the comparison with Fig. S3(b) uses the central Hall factors (not recalculated sample-specific eta) and neglects electron conduction. Two quadratures and an independent SPB integral identity are checked. This is an offline physics audit.
