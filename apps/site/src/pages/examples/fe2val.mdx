# Fe₂VAl₀.₉Si₀.₁: choosing the scattering mechanism

## Data

The full-Heusler compound Fe₂VAl₀.₉Si₀.₁, from the files
`01_Fe2VAl09Si01_see.txt` and `_rho.txt` of the desktop SeeBand: 30 points of
S and of ρ from 25 to 750 K. The SeeBand paper [[1]](#references) analyses the
same compound and cites its measurements to Garmroudi et al.
[[2]](#references). The [user guide](/guide/) walks through these files step
by step; this page is about one choice in it, the scattering mechanism.

## Choices

DPB, fit mode S + ρ, the default fit range of 50–800 K and no interpolation.
The fit was run three times, in this order:

1. alloy disorder, from the neural net's start (**Initial guess**);
2. ionized impurities, from the values the first fit left, without a new
   guess;
3. acoustic phonons, from the neural net's start.

## Result

| Scattering         | Status              | RMSE S    | RMSE ρ | $m_2/m_1$ | $E_g$    | $E_F$   | At a bound |
| ------------------ | ------------------- | --------- | ------ | --------- | -------- | ------- | ---------- |
| Acoustic phonons   | numerically settled | 2.68 µV/K | 9.8 %  | 1.32      | 0.05 eV  | 0.11 eV | none       |
| Alloy disorder     | numerically settled | 2.86 µV/K | 108 %  | 1.97      | 0.05 eV  | 0.10 eV | G = 2      |
| Ionized impurities | numerically settled | 4.58 µV/K | 138 %  | 2.63      | −0.14 eV | 0.01 eV | B = 1.1    |

The energies are as the fields show them, to 0.01 eV.

## Interpretation

S alone does not tell the mechanisms apart: all three describe it within
5 µV/K. ρ does. With acoustic phonons the model follows ρ within 10 %. With
alloy disorder it misses ρ by about its own size (RMSE 108 %) and G ends at its
bound. With ionized impurities it misses ρ by more (138 %), band 2 moves below
band 1 ($E_g$ < 0, overlapping bands) and B ends at its bound. Of the three,
only acoustic phonons describe both quantities, and with them S and ρ give a
small gap of about 0.05 eV with $E_F$ about 0.11 eV, in the conduction band.

A good description of S and ρ does not prove the band picture: other
parameter sets may describe the data as well, and this page did not search
for them.

## Limits

- With acoustic phonons the model ρ falls towards 0 at low temperatures, so it
  cannot follow the measured residual resistivity below about 100 K.
- Adding $R_H$ does not confirm these values. In the [user guide](/guide/), the
  S + ρ + $R_H$ fit with acoustic phonons reaches its limit of 20 iterations
  with an RMSE of $R_H$ of 104 %.
- The band diagram on the fit screen is a sketch (known issue
  `band_diagram_formula`, [#108](https://github.com/pdasya/seeband-web/issues/108)).
- Uncertainties are not assessed, and a physicist has not confirmed these
  results.

## Reproduce

1. On **Model fit**, choose **DPB** and **S + ρ**, and load
   `01_Fe2VAl09Si01_see.txt` and `_rho.txt`, choosing S and ρ for their
   second columns.
2. Keep the fit range of 50–800 K and **Interpolation** off.
3. Run the three fits in the order under [Choices](#choices).

The script `apps/web/scripts/capture-examples.mjs` in the repository runs these
steps in the app and prints the numbers.
