# Fe₂VAl₀.₉Si₀.₁: choosing the scattering mechanism

## Data

The full-Heusler compound Fe₂VAl₀.₉Si₀.₁, from the files
[`01_Fe2VAl09Si01_see.txt` and `_rho.txt`](https://github.com/xAngelswordx/SeeBand/tree/803b0e7a425a0a3f4f59688df77e3ad283ca49a1/data/Examples)
of the desktop SeeBand: 30 points of S and of ρ from 25 to 750 K, evenly
spaced. The SeeBand paper [[1]](#references) analyses the same compound and
cites its measurements to Garmroudi et al. [[2]](#references). The
[user guide](/guide/) walks through these files step by step; this page is
about one choice in it, the scattering mechanism.

## Choices

DPB, fit mode S + ρ, and otherwise the
[defaults](/examples/#reading-these-pages). The fit was run three times on one
page, in this order, each from the start shown:

| Fit | Scattering         | Start                                                                                                            |
| --- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Alloy disorder     | **Initial guess**: $m_2/m_1$ = 1.57, $E_g$ = 0.05 eV, $E_F$ = 0.10 eV; F = 1000 and G = 1, the defaults          |
| 2   | Ionized impurities | no guess, as the net has none for this mechanism: the band values fit 1 left; A = 40 000 and B = 1, the defaults |
| 3   | Acoustic phonons   | **Initial guess** for the band values; A = 14 099 and B = 1.1, which fit 2 left                                  |

## Result

| Scattering         | Status              | RMSE S    | RMSE ρ | $m_2/m_1$ | $E_g$    | $E_F$   | At a bound                     |
| ------------------ | ------------------- | --------- | ------ | --------- | -------- | ------- | ------------------------------ |
| Acoustic phonons   | numerically settled | 2.68 µV/K | 9.8 %  | 1.32      | 0.05 eV  | 0.11 eV | none; B = 1.097, its bound 1.1 |
| Alloy disorder     | numerically settled | 2.86 µV/K | 108 %  | 1.97      | 0.05 eV  | 0.10 eV | G = 2                          |
| Ionized impurities | numerically settled | 4.58 µV/K | 138 %  | 2.63      | −0.14 eV | 0.01 eV | B = 1.1                        |

The energies are as the fields show them, to 0.01 eV.

## Interpretation

S alone does not tell the mechanisms apart: all three describe it with an RMSE
below 5 µV/K. ρ does. With acoustic phonons the model follows ρ with an RMSE of
10 %. With alloy disorder the RMSE of ρ is 108 %, as large as ρ itself, and G
ends at its bound. With ionized impurities it is 138 %, and B ends at its
bound.

So, with the default bounds and these starts, only acoustic phonons describe
both quantities, and B then ends at 1.097, just inside its bound of 1.1. In
the sequential method the band values come from the fit of S; ρ adds only the
scattering amplitudes A and B. The fit of S gives a small gap of about 0.05 eV,
with $E_F$ about 0.11 eV, so about 0.06 eV inside band 2, the conduction band.

With ionized impurities, band 2's edge ends 0.14 eV below the top of band 1
($E_g$ < 0, the bands overlap), with $E_F$ within about 0.01 eV of the top of
band 1.

A good description of S and ρ does not prove the band picture: other
parameter sets may describe the data as well, and this page did not search
for them.

## Limits

- B's default bounds are 0.9–1.1. B or G ends at or just inside a bound in
  every fit here, so band 2's share of the scattering is largely set by the
  bounds.
- The ionized-impurity fit is provisional: whether band 2's weight should be
  N/R or N·R for this mechanism is an open question
  ([#247](https://github.com/pdasya/seeband-web/issues/247)), and it started
  from the alloy-disorder result.
- With acoustic phonons the model ρ falls towards 0 at low temperatures: with
  $E_F$ inside band 2, σ grows as 1/T, and the model has no
  temperature-independent scattering. So it cannot follow the measured
  residual resistivity, and the points from 50 to about 100 K, inside the fit
  range, count against it.
- Adding $R_H$ does not confirm these values. In the [user guide](/guide/), the
  S + ρ + $R_H$ fit with acoustic phonons reaches its limit of 20 iterations
  with an RMSE of $R_H$ of 104 %.
- Uncertainties are not assessed, and a physicist has not confirmed these
  results.

## Reproduce

1. Open **Model fit**, choose **DPB**, then **S + ρ**.
2. Load `01_Fe2VAl09Si01_see.txt` and choose S for its second column; load
   `01_Fe2VAl09Si01_rho.txt` and choose ρ.
3. Choose **Alloy disorder** under **Scattering**, press **Initial guess**,
   then **Run fit**.
4. Choose **Ionized impurities** and press **Run fit**, without a guess.
5. Choose **Acoustic phonons**, press **Initial guess**, then **Run fit**.

Check the start in the parameter fields against the table under
[Choices](#choices) before each run.
