# Si:P: a gap outside the default bounds

## Data

Phosphorus-doped silicon, from the files `02_SiP_see.txt`, `_rho.txt` and
`_hall.txt` of the desktop SeeBand: 12 points of S from 308 to 650 K, 19 of ρ
from 338 to 654 K and 10 of $R_H$ from 304 to 654 K. The SeeBand paper
[[1]](#references) analyses Si:P and cites its measurements to Agrawal, de Boor
and Dasgupta [[2]](#references), and gives a gap of about 1.08 eV from its own
two-band fit.

No neural net covers 308–650 K, so **Initial guess** offers no start.

## With the default bounds

DPB, fit mode S + ρ, acoustic phonons, the default start and bounds. The
bounds of $E_g$ and $E_F$ are ±0.26 eV (±3000 K), a quarter of the gap of
silicon.

| Status      | RMSE S   | RMSE ρ | $E_g$             | $E_F$                   | Also at a bound |
| ----------- | -------- | ------ | ----------------- | ----------------------- | --------------- |
| fit stalled | 545 µV/K | 52 %   | 0.26 eV, at bound | 0.01 eV, not determined | B = 1.1         |

This fit is a failure, and the screen says so: the status, $E_g$ at its bound,
and a caution that $E_F$ lies in the gap. It does not mean the two-band model
cannot describe Si:P; it means the gap it needs is outside the bounds.

## With wider bounds

**Edit bounds** sets $E_g$ to 0.5–1.5 eV and $E_F$ to −1.5–1.6 eV. The start is
then typed in: $m_2/m_1$ = 0.74, $E_g$ = 1.08 eV and $E_F$ = 1.0805 eV, 6 K
inside the conduction band. The start matters. In S + ρ + $R_H$:

- with $E_F$ exactly at the band edge, 1.08 eV, or 0.01 eV above it, the fit
  fails: its derivative cannot be verified at the start;
- **Legacy sequential** from those starts does finish, but with $m_1$ and A at
  their upper bounds and $E_F$ deep in the gap. It is not used here.

| Fit mode      | Status              | RMSE S    | RMSE ρ | RMSE $R_H$ | $m_2/m_1$ | $E_g$   | $m_1$   | At a bound |
| ------------- | ------------------- | --------- | ------ | ---------- | --------- | ------- | ------- | ---------- |
| S + ρ         | numerically settled | 54.7 µV/K | 23.8 % |            | 0.758     | 1.04 eV |         | B = 1.1    |
| S + ρ + $R_H$ | numerically settled | 54.7 µV/K | 23.8 % | 34.0 %     | 0.758     | 1.04 eV | 1.33 mₑ | B = 1.1    |

In both, $E_F$ ends 0.3 K above the conduction band edge. $R_H$ only sets $m_1$
here: the other values do not change when it is added.

## Interpretation

With room for it, the fit puts the gap at 1.04 eV, close to the 1.08 eV of the
paper, and describes the onset of intrinsic conduction in all three
quantities: the rise and fall of ρ and the steps in S and $R_H$. It is not a
close fit: S is off by 55 µV/K on average, most below 450 K, and ρ by 24 %.

## Limits

- The result depends on the start, which here came from the published gap.
  Without that knowledge, the default bounds and starts give the failed fit
  above.
- B ends at its upper bound, so B was set by the bound, not by the data.
- $E_F$ ends where it started, at the band edge. Whether the data determine it
  was not tested.
- S + ρ + $R_H$ settled after 2 iterations of its limit of 20.
- The band diagram on the fit screen is a sketch (known issue
  `band_diagram_formula`, [#108](https://github.com/pdasya/seeband-web/issues/108)).
- Uncertainties are not assessed, and a physicist has not confirmed these
  results.

## Reproduce

1. On **Model fit**, choose **DPB** and **S + ρ** (or **S + ρ + R_H**), load
   the `02_SiP_` files, and choose S, ρ and $R_H$ for their second columns.
2. Run the fit for the default-bounds result.
3. For the wider bounds, reload the page and the files, open **Edit bounds**,
   set the bounds above, type the start values into the parameter fields, and
   run the fit.

The script `apps/web/scripts/capture-examples.mjs` in the repository runs these
steps in the app and prints the numbers.
