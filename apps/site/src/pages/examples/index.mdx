# Worked examples

Three walkthroughs on the example sets that come with the desktop SeeBand, in
its [`data/Examples`](https://github.com/xAngelswordx/SeeBand/tree/803b0e7a425a0a3f4f59688df77e3ad283ca49a1/data/Examples)
folder. Each says which data were used, what was chosen, what the fit gave and
how far to trust it. The numbers and screenshots come from runs of this
version; [Results that changed](/results-changed/) says why a later version can
give others. The [user guide](/guide/) explains the screens.

- [Fe₂VAl₀.₉Si₀.₁: choosing the scattering mechanism](/examples/fe2val/). Two
  bands describe S and ρ; acoustic phonons describe ρ far better than the other
  mechanisms.
- [Si:P: a gap outside the default bounds](/examples/si-p/). Phosphorus-doped
  silicon has a gap of about 1 eV, which the default bounds of 0.26 eV cannot
  hold; with wider bounds the fit comes close to the published values.
- [AWI32: no mechanism describes everything](/examples/awi32/). S is described
  well with any mechanism, but none describes S, ρ and $R_H$ together, and the
  fitted parameters depend on the choice.

The example files are part of the desktop SeeBand, published under the MIT
licence. Where the SeeBand paper names the source of a set's measurements,
its page gives it.
