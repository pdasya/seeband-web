# AWI32: no fit describes everything

## Data

The sample AWI32, from the files
[`03_AWI32_SI_see.txt`, `_rho.txt` and `_hall.txt`](https://github.com/xAngelswordx/SeeBand/tree/803b0e7a425a0a3f4f59688df77e3ad283ca49a1/data/Examples)
of the desktop SeeBand: 50 points of S and of ρ from 4 to 561 K, and 50 of
$R_H$ from 303 to 527 K, each on evenly spaced temperatures. The desktop
SeeBand does not say what the sample is or where its measurements come from,
and neither does this page.

## Choices

DPB, fit mode S + ρ + $R_H$, and otherwise the
[defaults](/examples/#reading-these-pages); the fit range of 50–800 K takes
50–561 K of the data. The fit was run three times on one page, in this order,
each from the start shown:

| Fit | Scattering         | Start                                                                                                                 |
| --- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Acoustic phonons   | **Initial guess**: $m_2/m_1$ = 1.22, $E_g$ = 0.17 eV, $E_F$ = −0.03 eV; A = 40 000, B = 1, $m_1$ = 1 mₑ, the defaults |
| 2   | Ionized impurities | no guess, as the net has none for this mechanism: every value fit 1 left                                              |
| 3   | Alloy disorder     | **Initial guess** for the band values; F = 1000 and G = 1, the defaults; $m_1$ = 0.70 mₑ, which fit 2 left            |

## Result

| Scattering         | Status              | RMSE S    | RMSE ρ | RMSE $R_H$ | $m_2/m_1$ | $E_g$   | $E_F$    | $m_1$   | At a bound |
| ------------------ | ------------------- | --------- | ------ | ---------- | --------- | ------- | -------- | ------- | ---------- |
| Acoustic phonons   | numerically settled | 3.89 µV/K | 42.7 % | 23.6 %     | 1.39      | 0.16 eV | −0.02 eV | 1.96 mₑ | B = 1.1    |
| Ionized impurities | numerically settled | 3.62 µV/K | 76.5 % | 8.9 %      | 0.59      | 0.02 eV | −0.07 eV | 0.70 mₑ | B = 0.9    |
| Alloy disorder     | numerically settled | 3.84 µV/K | 28.2 % | 23.5 %     | 1.54      | 0.17 eV | −0.02 eV | 1.96 mₑ | none       |

The energies are as the fields show them, to 0.01 eV. In fit 3, $m_1$ moved
from its start of 0.70 mₑ to 1.96 mₑ, the value fit 1 reached from 1 mₑ.

## Interpretation

S does not choose: every mechanism describes it with an RMSE below 4 µV/K. ρ
and $R_H$ pull apart. Alloy disorder gives the lowest RMSE of ρ, still 28 %;
ionized impurities give the lowest of $R_H$, 9 %, with 77 % for ρ. None of
these fits describes all three quantities.

The parameters depend on the choice. With acoustic phonons and alloy disorder
the gap is about 0.16 eV, $E_F$ about 0.02 eV inside band 1, the valence band,
and $m_2/m_1$ 1.4–1.5; with ionized impurities the gap is 0.02 eV and $m_2/m_1$
0.59. Since none of the fits describes the data, none of these values should
be read as the band structure of the sample.

This is a valid result: these fits, with the default bounds and the starts
above, do not describe AWI32 over 50–561 K. Other bounds or starts were not
searched. Possible reasons, none tested here, include more than two bands,
mixed scattering, or a range too wide for one mechanism.

## Limits

- B ends at a bound with acoustic phonons and with ionized impurities, so the
  bound, not the data, set it there; B's default bounds are 0.9–1.1.
- Alloy disorder with $R_H$ carries the known issue `hall_dis_no_reference`
  ([#71](https://github.com/pdasya/seeband-web/issues/71)): the desktop fails
  this fit, and the web app uses other bounds of G, 0.5–2, so there is no
  desktop result to compare with.
- The ionized-impurity fit is provisional: whether band 2's weight should be
  N/R or N·R for this mechanism is an open question
  ([#247](https://github.com/pdasya/seeband-web/issues/247)). It also started
  from the acoustic-phonon result; another start may end elsewhere.
- Uncertainties are not assessed, and a physicist has not confirmed these
  results.

## Reproduce

1. Open **Model fit**, choose **DPB**, then **S + ρ + R_H**.
2. Load `03_AWI32_SI_see.txt`, `_rho.txt` and `_hall.txt`, choosing S, ρ and
   $R_H$ for their second columns.
3. With **Acoustic phonons** under **Scattering**, press **Initial guess**,
   then **Run fit**.
4. Choose **Ionized impurities** and press **Run fit**, without a guess.
5. Choose **Alloy disorder**, press **Initial guess**, then **Run fit**.

Check the start in the parameter fields against the table under
[Choices](#choices) before each run.
