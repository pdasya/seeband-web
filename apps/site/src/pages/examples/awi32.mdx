# AWI32: no mechanism describes everything

## Data

The sample AWI32, from the files `03_AWI32_SI_see.txt`, `_rho.txt` and
`_hall.txt` of the desktop SeeBand: 50 points of S and of ρ from 4 to 561 K,
and 50 of $R_H$ from 303 to 527 K. The desktop SeeBand does not say what the
sample is or where its measurements come from, and neither does this page.

## Choices

DPB, fit mode S + ρ + $R_H$, the default fit range of 50–800 K (so 50–561 K of
the data) and no interpolation. The fit was run three times, in this order:

1. acoustic phonons, from the neural net's start (**Initial guess**);
2. ionized impurities, from the values the first fit left, without a new
   guess;
3. alloy disorder, from the neural net's start.

## Result

All three fits settled numerically.

| Scattering         | RMSE S    | RMSE ρ | RMSE $R_H$ | $m_2/m_1$ | $E_g$   | $E_F$    | $m_1$   | At a bound |
| ------------------ | --------- | ------ | ---------- | --------- | ------- | -------- | ------- | ---------- |
| Acoustic phonons   | 3.89 µV/K | 42.7 % | 23.6 %     | 1.39      | 0.16 eV | −0.02 eV | 1.96 mₑ | B = 1.1    |
| Ionized impurities | 3.62 µV/K | 76.5 % | 8.9 %      | 0.59      | 0.02 eV | −0.07 eV | 0.70 mₑ | B = 0.9    |
| Alloy disorder     | 3.84 µV/K | 28.2 % | 23.5 %     | 1.54      | 0.17 eV | −0.02 eV | 1.96 mₑ | none       |

The energies are as the fields show them, to 0.01 eV.

## Interpretation

S does not choose: every mechanism describes it within 4 µV/K. ρ and $R_H$
pull apart. Alloy disorder describes ρ best and still misses it by 28 %;
ionized impurities describe $R_H$ best and miss ρ by 77 %. No mechanism
describes all three quantities.

The parameters depend on the choice. Acoustic phonons and alloy disorder agree
on a gap of about 0.16 eV with $E_F$ 0.02 eV inside the valence band and
$m_2/m_1$ of 1.4–1.5; ionized impurities give a gap of 0.02 eV and $m_2/m_1$ of
0.59. Since none of the fits describes the data, none of these values should
be read as the band structure of the sample.

This is a valid result: the two-band model with a single scattering mechanism
does not describe AWI32 over 50–561 K. Possible reasons, none tested here,
include more than two bands, mixed scattering, or a range too wide for one
mechanism.

## Limits

- B ends at a bound with acoustic phonons and with ionized impurities, so it
  was set by the bound, not by the data.
- The ionized-impurity fit started from the acoustic-phonon result; another
  start may end elsewhere.
- The band diagram on the fit screen is a sketch (known issue
  `band_diagram_formula`, [#108](https://github.com/pdasya/seeband-web/issues/108)).
- Uncertainties are not assessed, and a physicist has not confirmed these
  results.

## Reproduce

1. On **Model fit**, choose **DPB** and **S + ρ + R_H**, load the
   `03_AWI32_SI_` files, and choose S, ρ and $R_H$ for their second columns.
2. Keep the fit range of 50–800 K and **Interpolation** off.
3. Run the three fits in the order under [Choices](#choices).

The script `apps/web/scripts/capture-examples.mjs` in the repository runs these
steps in the app and prints the numbers.
